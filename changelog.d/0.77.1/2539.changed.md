- **A `soup loop` canary is judged by what people say about its answers, not only by failed requests** (#2539): once a feedback log exists, the pre-wired loop compares the share of negative feedback (thumb down, rating 1-2, an edit) on canary answers with the stable answers of the same rollout. A canary more than 5 points worse is withdrawn (with `--autoroll-on-regress`) even when every request succeeded, and one is promoted only when both its requests and its ratings are fine. This needs at least 30 rated answers on each side, so the canary waits, and `soup loop watch` starts no new iteration, until people have rated that many; the iteration notes show the progress, and `soup loop rollback` ends a wait. A loop without a feedback log is judged by failed requests alone, as before.
