- A `task: prm` step no longer builds full-vocabulary logits that nothing reads: the PRM trainer's forward and the two forwards of the GRPO PRM reward (`prm_reward`) pass `logits_to_keep=1` where the model's `forward` takes it, so the LM head runs on the last position only and the `[batch, seq_len, vocab]` tensor (and Accelerate's fp32 copy of it) shrinks to `[batch, 1, vocab]`; the hidden states the reward head reads, the loss and every gradient are unchanged. A forward without the argument keeps the old call. The VRAM estimate behind the `soup train` pre-flight, `soup profile`, `soup plan` and the MCP `profile` tool charges a PRM step's `logits` term on one position, `vocab × batch × 6` bytes, except for the eight causal-LM model types whose forward has no `logits_to_keep` (`gemma4_assistant`, `gemma4_unified_assistant`, `musicgen`, `musicgen_melody`, `prophetnet`, `trocr`, `whisper`, `xlstm`) and remote-code bases (`auto_map` in `config.json`), which keep the full-sequence charge. At 2,048 tokens, batch 4 and a 32,000-token vocabulary the term drops from 1.57 GB to 0.77 MB (#2368 by @ybai08 in #2372).
