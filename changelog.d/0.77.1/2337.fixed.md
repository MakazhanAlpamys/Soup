- The VRAM estimate behind the `soup train` pre-flight, `soup profile`, `soup plan` and the MCP `profile` tool no longer charges a `task: prm` step the cross-entropy chain it never builds: the PRM trainer runs the causal-LM forward without labels and takes its loss from the reward head, so its `logits` term is now the logits tensor alone, 2 bytes an element (`LOGITS_TENSOR_BYTES_PER_ELEMENT`), where it was 12. At 2,048 tokens, batch 4 and a 32,000-token vocabulary that is 0.52 GB in place of 3.15 GB. Every other task with an LM head is charged as before (#2332 by @ybai08 in #2337).
