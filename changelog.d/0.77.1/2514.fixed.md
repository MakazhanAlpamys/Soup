- **`soup serve --backend transformers` streams a `"stream": true` reply while it is generated (#2508, #2514).** It used to generate the whole reply and only then split it into chunks, so a client saw nothing until generation ended. `generate()` now runs on a worker thread with a `TextIteratorStreamer`; the chunk fields, final chunk, `finish_reason`, usage and `[DONE]` are unchanged, and the joined deltas equal the non-streamed reply. Closing the stream stops generation after the current token. A request served with `--reasoning-parser`, a draft model, n-gram lookup or MoLE routing, and the `/v1/messages` stream, are still generated in full and then sent.
