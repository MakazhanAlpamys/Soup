- **Removed deprecated `awq` and `gptq` export and quantize formats and extras (#338, #1690, #1710, in #1951 by @Nari-Ab).** Both upstream projects (`autoawq` and `auto-gptq`) are archived and incompatible with `[train]`. `soup export --format awq` / `--format gptq` and `soup quantize --to awq` / `--to gptq` are now refused at the CLI with non-zero exit codes naming the remaining supported formats (`gguf`, `onnx`, `tensorrt`, `bitnet`, `tq1_0`, `torchao`, `gguf-ud`). `soup export` also lost `--bits`, `--group-size`, and `--calibration-samples`, so scripts passing them now exit 2 with `No such option`; `--calibration-data` is now read only by `--format gguf-ud`. The `[awq]` and `[gptq]` extras and related export helpers are removed in v0.77; users who still need AWQ or GPTQ export should remain on 0.76.x.
