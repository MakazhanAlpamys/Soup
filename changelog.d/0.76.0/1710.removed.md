- **Removed deprecated `awq` and `gptq` export and quantize formats and extras (#338, #1690, in #1710).** Both upstream projects (`autoawq` and `auto-gptq`) are archived and incompatible with `[train]`. `soup export --format awq` / `--format gptq` and `soup quantize --to awq` / `--to gptq` are now refused at the CLI with non-zero exit codes naming the remaining supported formats (`gguf`, `onnx`, `tensorrt`, `bitnet`, `tq1_0`, `torchao`, `gguf-ud`). The `[awq]` and `[gptq]` extras and related export helpers have been removed.
