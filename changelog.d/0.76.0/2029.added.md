- **`training.stream_expert_layout: per_expert` streams a DeepSeek-V3-family MoE one routed expert
  at a time from `soup train`** (default `fused`). The layer buffers then hold only the dense part, so
  the VRAM bound no longer grows with the expert count. Moonlight-16B LoRA on an 8 GB laptop card
  peaked at 1.0 GB of VRAM against 3.1 GB fused, at about 2.6x the step time. Other architectures are
  refused at setup, `moe_lora` is refused with it, and the VRAM pre-flight charges the expert in
  flight. (#2029)
