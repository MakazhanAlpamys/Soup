- **Non-finite eval-gate baselines are now refused at read, write, and gate boundaries instead of silently passing (#1773 in #1779).** `resolve_baseline`, `stamp_baseline_scores`, `write_baseline_file`, and `run_gate` now require scores to be finite numbers; `NaN`, `+inf`, and `-inf` (including JSON numeric overflows such as `1e999` and `-1e999`) raise a `ValueError` naming the offending task. In `soup eval gate`, invalid baselines exit with code 3 (`EXIT_USAGE_ERROR`) before evaluating tasks or reporting a verdict, rather than printing `GATE PASSED` or misdiagnosing positive overflow as a regression.
