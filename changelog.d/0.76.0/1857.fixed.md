- **`backend: mlx` refuses a training or validation split smaller than one batch before the
  model loads (#1851 by @dryn85-max in #1857).** mlx-lm trains and evaluates in whole batches,
  so a one-row validation split (the default `val_split: 0.1` on the bundled 10-row
  `alpaca_tiny.jsonl`) crashed at the first evaluation with `Dataset must have at least
  batch_size=2 examples`, after the model had loaded. The refusal names both numbers and the
  fixes: lower `training.batch_size`, raise `data.val_split`, or set `data.val_split: 0`.
  A config that crashed this way now stops at load instead. Try: `soup train --config
  soup.yaml` with `backend: mlx`, `data.train: examples/data/alpaca_tiny.jsonl` and
  `batch_size: 2`.
