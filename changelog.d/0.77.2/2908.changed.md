- `soup adapters merge` scales each input's delta by its weight as of v0.78.0, where it used the weight squared: `linear`, `dare` and `svd` applied the normalised weight to both `lora_A` and `lora_B`, so `--weights 0.7,0.3` kept 0.49 and 0.09 of the two deltas and the default equal weights kept a quarter of each of two inputs. Each factor now takes the square root of the weight, the rule `soup adapters arithmetic` uses, so the same command keeps 0.7 and 0.3, and half of each. Migration: the numbers of every `linear`, `dare` and `svd` merge of two or more inputs change (an equal-weight pair doubles each input's own term, 0.25 to 0.5), and the weights printed by an earlier `--strategy cmaes` run reproduce a different adapter, so re-run merges whose output you compare or ship. `ties` is unchanged (#2908 by @abdulwaarith0).
