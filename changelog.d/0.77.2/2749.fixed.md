- Layer-streaming VRAM pre-flight now charges two retained cuBLAS workspaces. The estimate uses the active device default or `CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG`, and prints the charge. On the measured RTX 3050 grid this raises the total prediction to 101.04–106.57% of measured peak (the added term is 0.18–5.99% of peak); the 13.5 MB fixed allowance was measured before the workspace charge, so some configurations that passed on v0.77.2 may now be refused. Lower `training.batch_size` or `data.max_length`, or use packed NF4 backward when available. On compute capability 9–12 the default charge is 2 × 32 MiB; below 9 it is 2 × 8,320 KiB. When capability cannot be read, Soup uses the conservative 64 MiB total. (#1407 by @capyriver in #2749)
