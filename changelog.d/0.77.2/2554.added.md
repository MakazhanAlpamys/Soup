- **`soup loop` writes the iteration, version and progress logs** (#2554): the pre-wired loop now appends `.soup-loops/iterations.jsonl` (one line per iteration, and another whenever its canary is promoted or withdrawn), `versions.jsonl` (initial version, promotions, `soup loop rollback`) and `progress.jsonl` (each stage, and the rating progress of a waiting canary) in the documented loop formats, so an application can follow the loop without reading `loop.yaml`. Lines are only ever appended, one write per line, and a failed write becomes a note on the iteration instead of stopping the loop.
