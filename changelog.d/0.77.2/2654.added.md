- **Layer streaming trains data-parallel across processes and machines, BETA for `task: sft` (#2585, #2654).** Under `torchrun` or `accelerate launch` with one rank per machine, `soup train` now runs a `stream_layers: true` config instead of refusing it (#2586): Soup averages the LoRA gradients across the ranks itself, after the backward pass and before clipping. For machines that cannot reach each other's addresses (rented nodes behind a port mapping), plain non-distributed `soup train` processes form a TCP star around rank 0 when `SOUP_STREAM_DP_RANK`, `SOUP_STREAM_DP_WORLD_SIZE`, `SOUP_STREAM_DP_BIND_PORT` (rank 0) or `SOUP_STREAM_DP_ADDR` (the other ranks) and a shared `SOUP_STREAM_DP_TOKEN` are set: Soup shards the rows by rank, rank 0 averages the gradients weighted by each node's token count and alone writes the adapter, and a peer that dies stops the others at once with a `PeerClosedError` that names its rank (a silent one after the 300 s group timeout). There is no new config key, and settings the nodes would decide differently on are refused by name. Measured on two rented nodes: bitwise equal adapters on both, and no speed-up (5.4 times slower per step on a 12.6 ms link). See "Data-parallel streaming across machines" under Layer Streaming in `docs/performance-and-quantization.md`.
