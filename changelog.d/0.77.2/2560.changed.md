- **New `soup loop` loops train sft on corrections by default** (#2560): `soup loop init` now defaults to `--train-method sft` (existing `loop.yaml` files keep their method). SFT trains on corrected answers and on answers rated good; an answer that was only rated bad gives no row, and an edit always decides its answer for every method, whatever thumbs come later. With a feedback log, training starts at 8 new edits or 30 new rated answers (`--min-new-edits`, `--min-new-rated`). The sft recipe (target modules, LoRA r/alpha, lr, epochs, micro-batch, gradient accumulation, seed, quantization) is configurable in `loop.yaml` and through `soup loop init --sft-*`; its default (all linear modules, r 64 / alpha 128, lr 5e-4, 8 epochs, micro-batch 2, gradient accumulation 4, seed 42, every value written into the training config so none is inherited) is the one measured best, and `quantization: auto` trains without quantization when the base fits in free VRAM.
