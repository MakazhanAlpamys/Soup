- **New `soup loop` loops train sft on corrections by default** (#2560): `soup loop init` now defaults to `--train-method sft` (existing `loop.yaml` files keep their method). SFT trains on corrected answers and on answers rated good; an answer that was only rated bad gives no row, and an edit always decides its answer for every method, whatever thumbs come later. With a feedback log, training starts at 8 new edits or 30 new rated answers (`--min-new-edits`, `--min-new-rated`). The sft recipe (target modules, LoRA r/alpha, lr, epochs, micro-batch, gradient accumulation, seed, quantization) is configurable in `loop.yaml` and through `soup loop init --sft-*`; its default (all linear modules, r 64 / alpha 128, lr 2e-4, 8 epochs, micro-batch 2, gradient accumulation 4, seed 42, every value written into the training config so none is inherited) is the one measured best, and `quantization: auto` trains without quantization when the base fits in free VRAM. Migration: `soup loop init` without `--train-method` now creates an sft loop (the loop of v0.77.2 trained dpo on preference pairs) — pass `--train-method dpo` to keep preference-pair training. The default learning rate is 2e-4 (the A/B fit runs used 5e-4): in a study on one project (13 harvested rows, one seed, a 0.6B model), both runs at 5e-4 regressed a format suite by 0.10 to 0.175 with and without replay, and all five runs at 2e-4 or 1e-4 kept both quick suites within 0.05; whether the lower rate still learns the corrections as well was not measured. A `loop.yaml` that stores its own value keeps it and `--sft-lr` overrides (#2560).
