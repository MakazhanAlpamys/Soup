- `task: classifier`, `reranker`, `cross_encoder` and `asr` now full fine-tune on fp32 master weights, as every other full fine-tune does. They loaded the base with no `dtype`, so transformers 5 kept the checkpoint's own: bf16 for most decoder bases, fp16 for `whisper-large-v3` and `-large-v3-turbo`. AdamW then stepped 16-bit weights with 16-bit state, and at the default lr every norm and most other weights stayed where they started: after 8 steps on an RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell, 100% of the norms and 69.7% of the attention and MLP weights were bit-identical. With `classifier_lora: true` under bf16/fp16 mixed precision, the classification head the adapter trains (`modules_to_save`) is now upcast to fp32 too. Migration: from v0.78.0 a default `classifier`, `reranker`, `cross_encoder` or `asr` run over a 16-bit checkpoint holds fp32 weights, gradients and optimizer state, twice the memory (the pre-flight already budgeted it, but a run that fit the card on 0.77.x can now run out of memory), saves an fp32 model and trains to different losses; for the old footprint, train an adapter with `classifier_lora: true` or `asr_lora: true`, or lower `training.batch_size`.
