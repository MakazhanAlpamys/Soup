- **`soup loop` paraphrases the prompts people corrected, and `soup serve` keeps the loop's own calls out of its logs** (#2573): before training, the pre-wired loop asks a model for two paraphrases of each corrected prompt (once per answer, reused by every later iteration), drops ones that repeat the prompt or give the answer away, and appends them to `.soup-loops/paraphrases.jsonl`; they are what the corrections check of `soup ship --gate loop-v2` answers and never become training data. The model is the loop's own `soup serve` by default or `soup loop init --paraphraser-endpoint/--paraphraser-model`. A `soup serve` request with `X-Soup-Internal: loop-check` and a valid tool token writes no conversation record or thumbs and is never routed to a canary.
