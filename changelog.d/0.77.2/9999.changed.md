- `soup_cli.utils.profiler.profile_from_config`, the function `soup profile`, the MCP `profile` tool and the train pre-flight share, is documented as the entry point for programs that want the VRAM estimate without running `soup train`, and takes two optional arguments. `seq_len` prices that many tokens per row instead of `data.max_length` (clamped to 64 up to `data.max_length`; a bool or non-int raises `TypeError`), because the estimate prices rows that fill the length it is given and training data with short rows peaks far lower: Qwen3-0.6B with LoRA r16 on rows of at most 79 tokens peaked at 2108 MiB allocated and 2462 MiB on the whole card of an RTX 5070 Laptop, against 10.19 GB estimated at `data.max_length` 1024 and 2.86 GB at the rows' own length (`benchmarks/evidence/vram-qwen3-0.6b-realised-length.json`). The estimate alone is below the device peak on three of eleven clean runs, so a caller keeps the pre-flight's 10% margin. `shape` gives the model's `hidden_size`, `num_layers`, `intermediate_size`, `vocab_size` and `params` when its `config.json` cannot be read, so the logits term is still priced (a parameter count alone is refused); a readable `config.json` wins. The result gains `seq_len_source` and `seq_len_requested` only when `seq_len` is passed, a note for each argument, and `shape_source: "caller"` for a supplied shape; with neither argument it is unchanged. The pre-flight and `soup profile` price what they priced (#9999).
