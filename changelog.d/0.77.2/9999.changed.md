At most 20,000 rows and 90 seconds are read: past either, or with no readable tokenizer, a model that ships its own code, or data that is not chat messages, the run is priced at `data.max_length` as before and the panel says why. The estimate: a bf16 LoRA `sft` run's overhead is the CUDA context (162 MiB), the cuBLAS workspace (64 MiB), TRL's entropy-metric temporaries and 1.1 times the fp32 logits tensor in place of 0.5 GB and 5% of the allocated terms; a Qwen3 base under bf16 LoRA is charged the fp32 input of its QK-norm (4 bytes an element of q and k, per token per layer); a tied output head that a checkpoint stores twice (Qwen3-0.6B and 1.7B) is counted once, as the model loads it.
