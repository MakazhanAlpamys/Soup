- **`soup ship --gate loop-v2` judges a continual-learning candidate by the corrections it learned** (#2570): with `--corrections` (the trained edits and paraphrases of their prompts) and `--held-out` (edits kept out of training), ship answers both with the production and the candidate version and scores each answer by how many of the words the correction added it contains. It ships only when the corrections were learned (a one-sided exact sign test over the edits, at least 5 that changed), the held-out edits did not get significantly worse, and the benchmark suites did not regress; the task win and the style judge are recorded but no longer decide. The evidence is written to `--output` in the loop formats. Without `--gate`, `soup ship` is unchanged.
