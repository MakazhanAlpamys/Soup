- **`training.prefix_tree`: training batches laid out as prefix trees (#2840).** Tokens that several examples begin with and that are not trained on are held once per row. Each row has a 4D attention mask (a token attends to itself and its ancestors) and `position_ids` equal to the depth in the tree. The loss and its gradient are those of the ordinary batch holding the same examples. A step runs the trees' tokens instead of the sum of the examples' lengths. A step is still at most `batch_size` rows of `data.max_length` tokens, so the option is allowed beside `stream_layers`. Before the first step the trainer checks the model on real training examples and refuses one that does not reproduce the ordinary batch's logits. The option covers `task: sft`, `modality: text`, a single process and the transformers backend. It is refused beside `packing`, `multipack`, `stream_loss_chunk`, `neftune_alpha`, `curriculum`, `data.format: raft`, `data.replay` and `data.interleave`. An epoch has fewer optimizer steps, so runs compare by wall time or examples seen. On an RTX 3050 Laptop 4 GB with Mistral-7B layer-streamed in NF4, tool calling with one 4-function schema went from 0.400 to 1.404 examples/s at 512-token rows. In one seed of that run, the held-out loss was 0.1340 against 0.1488 in 66 % of the training time. `benchmarks/gate-prefix-tree.md` has the other datasets, the 0.5B runs and what is not explained.
