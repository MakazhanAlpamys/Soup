`soup ship` selects native BF16 or FP16 for the requested CUDA card, including indexed devices on mixed-GPU hosts, and uses FP32 on CPU or without an available CUDA runtime. Both loaders, console output and verdict/evidence agree on that dtype; FP16 evidence replays in the full-precision family without importing torch. Calls to `cuda_supports_bf16()` without a device retain their current-device behavior. (#2409 by @AubakirovArman in #2669)
