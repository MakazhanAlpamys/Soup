- `soup adapters merge` (`linear`, `ties`, `dare`, `svd`, and the candidates `cmaes` scores) and the same-rank path of `soup adapters arithmetic` rescale each input's `lora_B` and `lora_embedding_B` to the scaling of the `adapter_config.json` they write (the first input's: `lora_alpha / r`, or `/ sqrt(r)` with `use_rslora`, after `rank_pattern` / `alpha_pattern`). An input trained with a different `lora_alpha` used to be decoded at the first input's scaling, so it contributed at the wrong strength and `"a + b"` and `"b + a"` gave different adapters. Merges whose inputs have different scaling now give a different adapter than before; merges whose inputs have equal scaling are bit-identical. (#2861 by @abdumutalipabay0 in #2977)
