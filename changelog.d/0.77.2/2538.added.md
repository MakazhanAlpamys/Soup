- **`soup loop` learns from thumbs, ratings and edits with dpo, kto or sft** (#2538): the pre-wired harvest reads the feedback log (`soup loop init --feedback-log`, default `.soup-loops/feedback.jsonl`, `SOUP_LOOP_FEEDBACK_LOG` overrides it), joins it to the logged answers by id, and builds three datasets: edits become DPO pairs (next to the pairs from serve traces and the thumbs database), thumbs, ratings and edits become KTO rows labelled good or bad, and edits become SFT answers. `soup loop init --train-method dpo|kto|sft` picks the one the loop trains on; the default stays `dpo`. `--min-new-pairs` counts new rows of that dataset.
