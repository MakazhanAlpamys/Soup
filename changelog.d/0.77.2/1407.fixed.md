Layer-streaming VRAM pre-flight now charges the cuBLAS workspaces retained by its forward and
autograd CUDA threads. The estimate uses the active device's PyTorch default or the sizes in
`CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG`, and prints the workspace charge with the budget. The RTX 3050 fit
grid is now documented as a measured scope rather than a general accuracy guarantee.
