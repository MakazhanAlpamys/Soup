- **A `soup loop` canary is judged by what people say about its answers, not only by failed requests** (#2539): once a feedback log exists, the pre-wired loop compares the share of negative feedback (thumb down, rating 1-2, an edit) on canary answers with the stable answers of the same rollout. A canary more than 5 points worse is withdrawn (with `--autoroll-on-regress`) even when every request succeeded, and one is promoted only when both its requests and its ratings are fine. The gap must also be significant (a one-sided z-test, z > 1.645), so a few dozen ratings that happen to differ by 5 points no longer withdraw a canary, and an `OK` at the minimum means no significant regression, not proven equal. This needs at least 30 rated answers on each side, so the canary waits, and `soup loop watch` starts no new iteration, until people have rated that many; the iteration notes show the progress, and `soup loop rollback` ends a wait. The wait has an end: `soup loop init --canary-max-wait` (default `24h`; `90m`, `24h`, `7d` or plain seconds, at most `30d`) is how long after the canary started its ratings may stay short, and by default (`--canary-on-timeout errors-only`) the canary is then decided on its request errors alone and the notes say `promoted on errors only - quality unverified`; `--canary-on-timeout rollback` withdraws it instead and `wait` never gives up. A loop without a feedback log is judged by failed requests alone, as before.
