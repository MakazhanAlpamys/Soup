- `soup migrate --from unsloth` reads `beta` and `loss_type` off the final trainer's config for every task. `beta` goes to the field the task's trainer passes to TRL as `beta` (`dpo_beta`, `ipo_tau`, `kto_beta`, `orpo_beta`, `bco_beta` or `grpo_beta`, and `dpo_beta` for online DPO), where an `ORPOConfig`, `BCOConfig`, `GRPOConfig` or `OnlineDPOConfig` beta used to be replaced by Soup's default; `DPOConfig(loss_type="ipo")` migrates to `task: ipo` instead of plain DPO; and a `beta` or `loss_type` with no Soup field is named in the "not migrated" warning instead of being dropped in silence. Migration: `soup migrate --from unsloth` now stops with exit 1 on a `DPOConfig` whose `loss_type` is neither `sigmoid` nor `ipo`, naming it (it used to migrate to `task: dpo`, which trains the sigmoid loss), writes `DPOConfig(loss_type="ipo")` as `task: ipo` with `ipo_tau`, and writes an explicit `beta` of an ORPO, BCO, GRPO or online-DPO config instead of Soup's default; a `soup.yaml` already written is not affected.
