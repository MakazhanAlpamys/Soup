- `soup data filter --field NAME` no longer scores a row on its other fields when the row
  lacks `NAME`. A `NAME` that no row has now exits 1 before scoring or writing, with the
  wording `soup data dedup` uses; it used to filter every row on all of its fields and exit
  0. A row that lacks `NAME`, or holds `null` there, is left out of the filtered output and
  counted in the summary (`3 -> 1 rows (-2 removed; 1 has no 'output')`) after a warning,
  and `--score-only` writes it with `null` scores; the averages cover the scored rows only.
  A script that relied on a misspelt or partial `--field` falling back to the whole row
  should pass the field the rows hold, or omit `--field` (#3437 in #3490).
