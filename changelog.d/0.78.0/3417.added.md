- `training.l2l` now runs DeepSeek-V3-family MoE models streamed with `stream_expert_layout: per_expert`: the launch reads every routed expert through one bounded expert buffer pool (its arena is charged in the VRAM pre-flight in place of the single in-flight expert, and its host staging in the disk-tier staging check), the L2L step reads each hit expert in the forward, again in the recompute and in its own backward, a routing guard refuses a recompute that routed tokens differently from its forward, and the step equals gradient accumulation bit for bit on CPU in fp32 and under bf16 autocast. A MoE model with the fused layout is refused at launch, before any weight is sharded, naming `stream_expert_layout`. New keys `l2l_two_read_experts` and `l2l_grouped_expert_routes` (off by default; refused unless `l2l` is true and the layout is `per_expert`, and a bool only) turn on two-read experts and grouped route dispatch; both are recorded in `l2l_recipe.json` and a resume with another value is refused. `l2l_auto_k` stays refused on a MoE model. Dense runs and MoE runs without `l2l` behave as before. (#3417 by @Batyrkhantzy111, @M1kado0, @Artix6034)
