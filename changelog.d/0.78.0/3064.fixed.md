- `soup sweep --early-stop` now takes a factor of 1.0 or more and refuses `nan` in any spelling, as a usage error that names the option (exit 2), before the config is read and under `--dry-run` too. The check is `loss > best_loss * factor`, and the best loss already includes the arm that just finished. A factor below 1 therefore fired on the best arm itself, so the sweep stopped after its second arm even when every arm improved. A negative factor did the same. `nan` turned early stopping off without a word. Unset, `1.0` and above, and `inf` behave as before. The `nan` refusal now words a one-sided range the way Click does (`x>=1.0`). Migration: `--early-stop 0` and `--early-stop nan` used to turn early stopping off, and a factor below 1 stopped the sweep after its second arm; omit the flag, or pass 1.0 or more (#3006 by @Keshemir in #3064).
