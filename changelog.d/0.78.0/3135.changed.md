- `soup adapters diff` compares each LoRA module on its effective delta `ΔW = scaling · B @ A`, with the scaling from each adapter's own `adapter_config.json` (`lora_alpha / r`, or `/ sqrt(r)` with `use_rslora`, after `rank_pattern` / `alpha_pattern`; 1.0 without a usable `r` or `lora_alpha`, as in `soup adapters check`). It compared the raw `lora_A` and `lora_B` factors, so a `lora_alpha` change read as no change, adapters of different rank shared no row, and a re-parameterised adapter with the same ΔW read as a large change. Linear and embedding factor pairs are compared this way; a conv pair, a half pair, a DoRA magnitude vector and `modules_to_save` weights are still diffed by name. Migration: in `--format json`, `per_layer` and `top_changed` name a module (`…self_attn.q_proj`) instead of its `.lora_A.weight` / `.lora_B.weight` tensors, `shared_layers` counts modules, `norm_a` and `norm_b` are the norms of each module's ΔW (`frobenius` and `relative` those of their difference), and `effective_rank_a` and `effective_rank_b` are the mean effective rank of each module's ΔW and of every other 2-D tensor.
