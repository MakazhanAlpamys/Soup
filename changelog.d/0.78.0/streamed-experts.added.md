- `StreamedExperts` can now read its routed experts through the bounded `ExpertBufferPool` as well as an `ExpertFetcher`: a pooled module validates routing and the stateless SiLU activation, leases each hit expert in the forward and again in the backward (first-order only), keeps a per-visit `last_trace`, and hints the next expert to the pool's read-ahead. Opt-in levers, off by default and available on either source: `grouped_expert_routes` (one `nonzero` finds the hit experts and their rows) and `fast_row_gather` (light autograd gathers, bit-equal to indexing for the unique rows of one expert). `install_expert_streaming` validates every layer before replacing any, and `install_streaming` / `build_streamed_model` take `expert_pool`, `grouped_expert_routes` and the pool's `expert_transfer`, `expert_host_cache_bytes` and `expert_prefetch`; a run that sets none of them behaves as before.
