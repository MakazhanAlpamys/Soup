- `eval.custom` with `scoring: semantic` now reads a word without its leading and trailing punctuation, so `h2o.` and `(h2o)` are the word `h2o`. A word that is only punctuation is dropped, and punctuation inside a word (`3.14`, `don't`) stays part of it. The scorer is still the lexical Jaccard overlap of words, with a match at 0.5. A row's similarity can change in either direction on the same answers (`a. a x` against `a. a y` was 0.5, a match, and is now 1/3). Saved results are not recomputed. (#3120)
