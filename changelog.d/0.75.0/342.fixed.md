- **GRPO `variant=None` no longer silently corrupts adapters on large log-ratio overflow.**
  `torch.exp(log_ratio)` overflowed to `inf` at padding positions where current and
  old-policy log-probabilities diverged by more than ~88, and PyTorch's `ExpBackward` then
  computed `0.0 × inf = NaN`. The forward loss stayed finite (PPO clip and completion mask
  absorbed the `inf`), but the NaN gradient propagated into the optimizer step and
  irreversibly corrupted all adapter weights. The fix clamps old-policy log-probabilities
  (`old_per_token_logps`) before they reach the loss, preventing the overflow without affecting
  normal-range training (bit-identical for all `|dlogP| < 20`). A pre-step gradient
  watchdog now also detects any remaining non-finite gradients and zeros them before
  `optimizer.step()`, preserving the adapter and reporting the cumulative skip count in
  the run record. (#342)
