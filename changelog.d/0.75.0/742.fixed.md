- **Distillation no longer multiplies its gradient by `gradient_accumulation_steps` (#720 by @dchaudhari7177 in #742).** `_DistillTrainer.compute_loss` returns a mean over its own microbatch and ignores `num_items_in_batch`, but Transformers classifies an ordinary causal LM as consuming loss kwargs and therefore skips its own accumulation compensation. So the same effective batch split four ways produced four times the gradient, changing the effective learning rate whenever accumulation was on. The trainer now sets `model_accepts_loss_kwargs = False` after `Trainer.__init__`, as the Transformers docstring prescribes for a `compute_loss` that does not use `num_items_in_batch`. `tests/test_issue720_distill_grad_accum.py` runs the real `_DistillTrainer` body through `Trainer.training_step` on a one-layer Llama and asserts the accumulated gradient matches the unaccumulated one at 4 and 8 steps.
