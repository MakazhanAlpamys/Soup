- **Hardware-fit gate correctly infers Hub model sizes using boundary-safe regex and MoE parsing (#1198 by @Areen-09 in #1285).** `utils/gpu.py:model_size_from_name` previously relied on a static scan of hardcoded substring markers and fell back to 7.0B, causing VRAM under-prediction up to ~58x–96x on large models (such as 72B, 32B, 14B, 405B, 675B) and substring collision bugs (e.g., `11b` matching `1b`, `17b`/`27b` matching `7b`, `0.8b` matching `8b`, and `8x7b` matching `7b`). Model sizes are now dynamically parsed with boundary-safe regex, active-parameter tokens (`-aNNb`) are filtered to retain total parameters, and MoE architectures (`8x7B`, `8x22B`, generic `NxMb`) are supported.
