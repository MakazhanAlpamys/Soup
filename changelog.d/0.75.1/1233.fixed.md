- **Tokenize non-Latin scripts, non-spaced languages, and digits in `soup diagnose` and `live_eval` (#1233 by @kok-o).** The previous regex discarded all non-ASCII characters and standalone digits, causing healthy models responding in non-Latin scripts (Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, CJK, etc.) to falsely read as MAJOR mode collapse, and causing forgetting probes using `token_f1` to evaluate to zero retention. Tokenization now performs NFC Unicode normalization, preserves non-Latin word boundaries and combining marks, generates character bigrams for non-spaced scripts (CJK, Kana, Thai), and accurately scores cross-script mode collapse, memorization, and forgetting.
