- **GRPO's `accuracy` and verifiable `math` rewards now read the gold answer the way they read the completion (#1226 in #1316).** An Alpaca `output` or a ShareGPT/ChatML reference turn such as `6*7=42\n#### 42` was compared raw, so every correct completion scored 0.0, every group had zero reward variance and GRPO got no signal, with no warning. Both sides now go through one parser: the text after the last `####`, the last `\boxed{}` (a space before the brace and nested braces allowed), or the text after `The answer is` / `Answer:` (also `**Answer**:`, and on the next line), whose number is read from its own clause, so `The answer is 41 apples, not 42.` is 41. An answer phrase that names more than one distinct value is a hedge and states no answer: `The answer is either 41 or 42.` scores 0.0 in both rewards whatever the gold, and a gold written that way is refused (the same value twice, as in `42 (i.e. 42.0)`, is not a hedge). Every number in the clause counts, so `The answer is 42 because 6*7=42.` is a hedge too, while `The answer is 42, because 6*7=42.` reads 42; a time or a ratio such as `3:45` or `1:1,000` is one answer, compared as text. `$`, `\(...\)`, trailing punctuation, thousands separators such as `1,000` and `1{,}000`, and the Unicode minus are normalised. A numeric gold is compared by value; any other gold, such as `\frac{14}{3}`, is compared as normalised text (case, whitespace, `\left` / `\right` and `\dfrac` ignored), now by `math` as well, so a dataset that mixes numeric and LaTeX golds, such as MATH-500, loads under `math`, and validation prints how many golds are compared as text. `accuracy` no longer pays 0.5 when the gold merely appears in the completion (that paid `#### 420` for a gold of 42) and scores only 0.0 or 1.0; a completion with no marked answer is scored by its last number, so `Six times seven is 42.`, and also `Not 42.`, now score 1.0 instead of 0.5. `math`'s 0.6 near-miss band is unchanged. A row whose gold states no single final answer, such as a multi-line reference with no marked answer, a hedge, or a `#### Solution` heading over a worked solution (a `####` line that is not a number and has more text after it), is refused before generation with its split, row and field and a count of the other such rows. PPO's `accuracy` and `math` rewards use the same scoring, but PPO's data is not validated this way (on the supported trl, PPO scores rollouts with its reward model).
