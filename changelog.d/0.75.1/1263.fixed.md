- **GRPO variants now record the `kl` divergence metric when `beta != 0`, matching TRL stock loss reporting (#1263).** All non-standard GRPO variants (`gspo`, `dapo`, `dr_grpo`, `bnpo`, `two_sided`, and `rft`) calculate the batch-mean per-token KL against the frozen reference model and record it under `self._metrics[mode]["kl"]` with cross-process gather. `rft` averages strictly over its accepted positive-advantage tokens, matching its loss denominator. When `beta == 0`, no metric entries are recorded.
