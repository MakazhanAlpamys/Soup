- **Two Fast-LoRA patchers on one model now compose by a stated precedence and
  unpatch back to the model they started from (#1165 by @ybai08 in #1617).**
  Which kernel ran on a projection two patchers wanted used to depend on which
  was patched first, and `unpatch_fast_lora_single_projection` left a bound
  `forward` in the instance `__dict__` of every projection it had patched. The
  group kernels now own their projections in either order: shared-X Q/K/V takes
  q/k/v and the fused SwiGLU MLP takes gate/up/down, releasing the
  single-projection kernel from them, and the single-projection kernel skips
  what a group owns. Unpatching any of the three, in any order, leaves no
  instance `forward` and no marker attribute behind, and unpatching a group
  kernel does not bring the single-projection kernel back on the projections
  it took. `test_qkv_refuses_projections_already_owned_by_single_projection`
  (#838) pinned the old first-patcher-wins order and now pins the takeover.
