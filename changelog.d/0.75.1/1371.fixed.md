- **`accuracy` and `verifiable/math` scored 0.0 on common MATH answer spellings (#1346 by @AmirF194 in #1371).** `normalize_answer` had no rule for `\text{}` / `\textbf{}` / `\mathrm{}` / `\mbox{}` wrappers, degree marks, or the compact `\frac12` / `\frac1{2}` / `\frac{1}2` spellings, and a phrase answer's one-letter variable prefix (`x = 7`) was read on the completion side but not the reference side. A correct completion in any of those spellings scored 0.0 against a gold in the other. All four are now handled inside the one shared normaliser both sides already go through. A variable name is kept rather than discarded: when both sides name one and the names differ (`x = 3` against `y = 3`), the pair still scores 0.0, since a directrix or an asymptote's variable is part of its answer. A side effect of running the degree and `\text{}` rewrites before the hedge scan: a clause such as `30^\circ, 60^\circ` now reads as two distinct values and hedges, where it previously read as one; this is a deliberate, pinned change, not a regression. Units are still not stripped and nothing is evaluated (`\frac{1}{2}` does not equal `0.5`); see `docs/training.md`.
