- The layer-streaming sharder now decodes block-scaled FP8 source checkpoints (DeepSeek-V3 / Kimi K2 layout: e4m3 weights with a per-128x128 `weight_scale_inv`) before NF4, instead of casting the raw e4m3 bytes and copying the scales into the shard, which produced wrong weights without a warning. Float8 tensors without their scale or without an fp8 `quantization_config` are now refused by name. For such sources NF4 now shards without double-quant by default, and an explicit `bnb_4bit_use_double_quant: true` is refused: with it on, the bitsandbytes codec fails the per-block reconstruction gate on real DeepSeek-V3 weights (by @Lameda12 in #2).
