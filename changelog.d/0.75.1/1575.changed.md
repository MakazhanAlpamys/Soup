- **`soup ab` reaches `accept_h0` about 3 times sooner when there is no difference, and `--beta` now bounds wrong accepts under repeated looks (#1418 by @abdulwaarith0 in #1575).** The statistic and the `reject_h0` rule from #1265 are unchanged. `accept_h0` used to wait for the Bayes factor to fall to `log(beta / (1 - alpha))`, which took 621 pairs per arm at an `--effect-size` of 0.3 standard deviations (`--alpha 0.05`, `--beta 0.20`). It now comes once a confidence sequence for the difference, built from the same mixture and valid however often you re-run `soup ab`, lies inside `(-effect_size, +effect_size)`: 220 pairs at 0.3, 85 at 0.5 (was 281). The accept panel now says this: any difference is smaller than `--effect-size`, at confidence `1 - --beta`. This is not free. A true difference of exactly `--effect-size` now ends in `accept_h0` in up to 3.9% of simulated runs at `--beta 0.20` (1.8% at 0.10), against under 0.5% before, so the chance of detecting it falls from 0.997 to about 0.965; `--beta` is the bound, and a lower `--beta` buys back accuracy for more rows. The false-positive rate can only fall, and does (worst 0.033 to 0.027 at `--alpha 0.05`). Anyone already passing `--beta` gets the faster rule with no change to flags or configs; the `--alpha` + `--beta` < 1 check from #1339 stays, with its reason restated. The record is `benchmarks/gate-1418-ab-cs-accept.md`, and `docs/evaluation.md` has the new time-to-accept table.
