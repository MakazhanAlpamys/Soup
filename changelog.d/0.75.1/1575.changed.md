- **`soup ab` reaches `accept_h0` about twice as soon when there is no difference, for every user and at the defaults, and `--beta` is retired (#1418 by @abdulwaarith0 in #1575).** The statistic and the `reject_h0` rule from #1265 are unchanged. `accept_h0` used to wait for the Bayes factor to fall to `log(beta / (1 - alpha))`, which took 621 pairs per arm at an `--effect-size` of 0.3 standard deviations (`--alpha 0.05`). It now comes once a confidence sequence for the difference, built from the same mixture at level `--alpha` and valid however often you re-run `soup ab`, lies inside `(-effect_size, +effect_size)`: 307 pairs at 0.3, 117 at 0.5 (was 281). This changes when every `soup ab` run says `accept_h0`, with no flag to opt out, and so when the `--slack-url` and `--discord-url` webhooks fire with `"decision": "accept_h0"`; a pipeline that waits on that message will hear it sooner. The accept panel now says what it means: any difference is smaller than `--effect-size`, at confidence `1 - --alpha`. A true difference of exactly `--effect-size` ends in `accept_h0` in at most 0.0083 of simulated runs at `--alpha 0.05` (was 0.0042) and 0.0019 at 0.01 (was 0.0032), so the chance of detecting it stays within 0.005 of before; the false-positive rate can only fall, and does. The `log_likelihood_ratio` reported next to an `accept_h0`, in the table and the webhook payload, is still the reject rule's statistic and can be positive. `--alpha` now bounds both wrong verdicts, so `--beta` no longer does anything: for this release `soup ab --beta X` prints that, says a stricter accept means a lower `--alpha`, and runs exactly as without it, and `MsprtConfig(beta=...)` raises the same message as a `SoupConfigDeprecationWarning`; the next release refuses it. The `--alpha` + `--beta` < 1 check from #1339 went with it. The record is `benchmarks/gate-1418-ab-cs-accept.md`, and `docs/evaluation.md` has the new time-to-accept table.
