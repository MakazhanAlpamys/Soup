- **`mistral-medium-3-5-sft` can now load and attach LoRA (#1395 by @Areen-09 in #1482).** The recipe base model (`mistralai/Mistral-Medium-3-5`) uses `Mistral3ForConditionalGeneration`, a vision-language wrapper. The recipe was missing `modality: vision` under `task: sft`, causing the loader to dispatch to `AutoModelForCausalLM` and fail. In addition, `target_modules: auto` had no mapping for `mistral3` in `MOE_TEXT_LORA_TARGETS`, which now targets the language model attention projections (`.*language_model\..*\.self_attn\.(q_proj|k_proj|v_proj|o_proj)`) while excluding the vision tower, multimodal projector, and MLP projections. The recipe is also unpinned from the cannot-attach exceptions list in `scripts/check_recipe_attach.py`.
