- **An untied streamed checkpoint issues the head's load right after the embedding lookup instead of at the last decoder layer (#1174 by @VanshSharmaPES in #1258).** The embedding's bytes in the shared large-layer slot are dead once the lookup has run, so `StreamPrefetcher` now fires `tail_prefetch` from layer 0's `advance()` (`head_prefetch_layer`, default 0) rather than the last layer's, giving the head's copy every decoder layer's forward to hide behind instead of one. `head_prefetch_layer = None` restores the old timing. Loss, gradients and adapter weights are bit-identical between the two timings, and the sequence and count of real large-layer loads are unchanged; the refill is ordered after the queued lookup by the `wait_stream` in `LargeLayerBufferPool.load_async`, which a new GPU test pins. The speed effect is not measured yet: it needs the step's total `wait()` on a GPU, on both the RAM and the disk tier. Tied checkpoints have no shared slot and are unchanged.
