- **GRPO's `accuracy` and verifiable `math` rewards now end the answer clause at justification connectives `because` and `since` (#1328).** Previously, an inline justification such as `The answer is 42 because 6*7=42.` stayed in the clause and counted all numbers, treating the answer as a hedge and scoring 0.0. The clause now ends at `because` or `since`, correctly reading 42.
