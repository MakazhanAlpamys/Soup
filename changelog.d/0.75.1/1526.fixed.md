- **`soup diagnose` live probes now read ShareGPT, Alpaca `input`, ChatML, tool-calling, DPO, KTO and plaintext datasets (#1435 by @bakdauletbaktygaliyev in [#1526](https://github.com/MakazhanAlpamys/Soup/pull/1526)).** Rows go through the same format converters training uses, with the old flat-key lookup as a fallback, so these datasets give the forgetting, format and mode-collapse probes a prompt/answer pair instead of `NOT_RUN`. Memorization no longer waits for pairs and runs on plaintext rows. LLaVA and audio rows are skipped because the probes are text-only, a prompt over 8192 characters is skipped instead of failing the probe, the `NOT_RUN` reason names the dominant cause, and skipped rows are counted in `extras.rows_skipped` and printed as `Rows skipped: ...`. For multi-turn ChatML rows the probe prompt now stops at the first assistant reply.
