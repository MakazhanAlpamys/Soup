- A `task: grpo` run with `training.eval_steps` now records the held-out reward (`eval_reward`, higher is better) as its validation metric: the live panel shows a `Val reward` row, the tracker's metrics table gains a `val_reward` column (added to existing databases in place), and the training event stream carries `val_reward`. TRL's `eval_loss` on grpo, the policy objective, is no longer stored or shown as `val_loss`; every other task keeps `eval_loss` / `val_loss` unchanged. The per-task choice lives in `VALIDATION_METRICS` in `utils/eval_schedule.py` (#1592, #1389).
