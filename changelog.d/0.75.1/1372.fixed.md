- **`backend: unsloth` is refused at load on the tasks that have no unsloth setup (#1357 by @Srinivasan8888 in #1372).**
  `reward_model`, `prm`, `classifier`, `reranker`, `cross_encoder`, `unlearn` and `moe_lora_routing` accepted `backend: unsloth`, but their trainers never read it: the run trained on plain transformers while `soup train` printed "unsloth (fast mode)", and `soup doctor --config` exited 0. The config now stops at load, naming the task and pointing to `backend: transformers`, and `soup doctor --config` exits 2. `distill`, `asr` and `online_dpo` keep their own existing refusals, and the docs no longer say unsloth works with every task.
