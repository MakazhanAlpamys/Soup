- `info_rm` reward-hacking detection no longer votes on a reward too discrete for its median split. On a two-valued reward (e.g. `reward_fn: accuracy`'s 0/1) every split left a constant half, so the pooled-variance floor collapsed to `1e-9` and a first step near a 50% success rate recorded a phantom separation of ~31,623 as the baseline — later, healthier steps then read as a collapse and could halt the run or mutate β. Those steps now cast no vote and log one warning naming the cause; `halt_on_hack`/`kl_control` state is untouched, and the continuous-collapse path is unchanged. The pooled-variance floor is also tied to the observed reward range instead of a bare `1e-9`. Detector logs and `mitigation_log.jsonl` from runs before this version are not comparable — use `reward_hack_detector: rm_ensemble` or the length/repetition signals on two-valued rewards (#1438 by @Momoyeyu in #1630).
