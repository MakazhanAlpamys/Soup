- **`training.packing: true` preserves assistant-only loss mask during SFT instead of training prompt/system tokens (#1236).** In TRL 0.29.1, dataset packing drops the unpreserved `labels` column and falls back to training all input tokens except the first. SFT datasets now emit an `assistant_masks` column alongside `labels`, which TRL packs alongside `input_ids` and uses to mask prompt/system tokens with `-100` during collation. Pre-tokenized caches carrying `labels` are also augmented with `assistant_masks` automatically.
