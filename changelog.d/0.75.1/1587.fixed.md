- **`training.reasoning_effort` and `training.train_on_eot` now load only on the tasks that read them (#806 by @dchaudhari7177 in #1587).**
  Both are applied by the SFT formatter, which runs on `sft`, `tts` and
  `distill`; `train_on_eot` also keys the `soup data preprocess` cache that
  `pretrain` loads. Before, the gate refused `tts`, where both flags take
  effect, and accepted `pretrain`, `classifier`, `reranker` and
  `cross_encoder`, where `reasoning_effort` (and, outside `pretrain`,
  `train_on_eot`) loaded and changed nothing. Such a config now fails at load.
