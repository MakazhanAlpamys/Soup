- The layer-streaming VRAM pre-flight now charges cuBLAS workspaces (#1407): a streamed step holds one workspace per handle-stream pair (two of them — the forward thread's and autograd's), up to 32 MiB each on Hopper/Blackwell cards, which `max_memory_allocated` counts but the formula-only estimate did not. The charge covers the excess over the 8,320 KiB-per-pair baseline already inside the fitted slack constant and honours `CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG`; the pre-flight panel shows it as a `cuBLAS` line when non-zero.
